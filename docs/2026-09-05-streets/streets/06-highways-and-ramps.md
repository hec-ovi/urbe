# 06. Highways and ramps

Status: drafted.

## Build

Reserve the complete highway or ramp envelope before placing buildings, fixtures, or connections. Derive supports, barriers, side bands, pedestrian routes, and navigation from the same path.

## Check

- clearance is valid for intended traffic;
- supports fit the street and sidewalk;
- the underside leaves usable space;
- ramps connect actual elevations and directions;
- buildings and fixtures do not intersect the envelope;
- collision and navigation agree with the render.
