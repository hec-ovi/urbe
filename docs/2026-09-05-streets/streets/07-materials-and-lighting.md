# 07. Materials and lighting

Status: drafted.

## Build

Keep road, curb, sidewalk, crossing, border, planting, and fixture surfaces as semantic regions with fitted mapping. Place lights and signals from road and crossing anchors.

## Check

- surface roles remain visually distinct;
- broad panels do not read as small repeated tiles;
- markings terminate inside their regions;
- local lighting reveals boundaries and roughness;
- signal faces point toward their users;
- fixtures clear paths and structures.
