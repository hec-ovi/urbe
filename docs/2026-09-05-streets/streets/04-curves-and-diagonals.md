# 04. Curves and diagonals

Status: drafted.

## Build

Use a declared angle or curvature profile. Derive both edges, curb, sidewalk, transition pieces, and mapping from the same path.

## Check

- join positions and tangents agree;
- width stays within the profile;
- inner and outer edges do not cross;
- texture distance remains stable;
- no abrupt kink, gap, overlap, or arbitrary crop appears.
