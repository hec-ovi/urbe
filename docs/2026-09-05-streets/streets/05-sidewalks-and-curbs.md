# 05. Sidewalks and curbs

Status: drafted.

## Build

Construct a sidewalk field above the road with a defined curb top and vertical face. Fit broad paving modules, borders, crossings, planting strips, and transitions from the sidewalk boundary.

## Check

- curb height is visible and numerically defined;
- panel joints use a shared origin;
- joints terminate at fitted boundaries;
- pedestrian width remains clear;
- transitions close corners without holes or overlaps;
- material regions follow the geometry.
